## Biography
-  **Tuesday** is one of the first [Slapstick](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick "Slapstick") family tree bands. After the [1996](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/1996 "1996") breakup, about half the members created an "anti-ska" band called Tuesday.

-  Dan Andriano (bass, vocals), Matt Stamps (guitar), and Rob Kellenberger (drums, vocals); 3 of 7 Slapstick members, were all inspired by _Fifteen_ and their political songwriting, which was evident in their early sound. They evolved somewhat, however, by the time they cut their first EP in [1997](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/1997 "1997") titled _Early Summer_, which was a pop and emo fusion fusion.

-  Tyler Wiseman joined on second guitar later in '97, and [Asian Man](https://punkdatabase.com/w/index.php?title=Asian_Man&action=edit&redlink=1 "Asian Man (page does not exist)") released their only LP before their hiatus, _Free Wheelin'_ which spawned their only U.S. tour. Tuesday broke up and Andriano, ex-Slapstick, replaced Rob Doran on bass and backup vocals with [Alkaline Trio](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Alkaline_Trio "Alkaline Trio"), another [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") band.

-  Kellenberger went on to play drums with [Duvall](https://punkdatabase.com/w/index.php?title=Duvall&action=edit&redlink=1 "Duvall (page does not exist)"), [Colossal](https://punkdatabase.com/w/index.php?title=Colossal&action=edit&redlink=1 "Colossal (page does not exist)"), and briefly for the reunited [Smoking Popes](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Smoking_Popes "Smoking Popes") all third generation [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") bands. Wiseman, not a Slapstick member went on to play with other third generation [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") band [The Honor System](https://punkdatabase.com/w/index.php?title=The_Honor_System&action=edit&redlink=1 "The Honor System (page does not exist)") aside other original Slapstick members and also played for and _Whale|Horse_.
