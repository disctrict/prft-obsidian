## Biography
Downfall was a ska and punk band from the Bay Area formed by Tim Armstrong (vocals), Matt Freeman (bass), Dave Mello (drums), Pat Mello (guitar), and Jason Hammon (guitar) following the break-up of [[Operation Ivy]] in 1989 and pre-dating Armstrong's and Freeman's wider recognition in [[Rancid]]. They performed three shows, one at 924 Gilman Street, over a period of three months.
