## Biography
The Salteens (punk) were a 1990s Minneapolis-based punk band, featuring members who later played in [[The Soviettes]], [[The Gateway District]], Cleveland Bound Death Sentence, and The Strike. They are known for raw, female-led punk sound, primarily recognized for their Whiskey & Records 7-inch EP.
