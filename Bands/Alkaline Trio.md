## Biography
The Alkaline Trio came together in 1996, one of the bands part of the [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") (Andriano was a [Slapstick](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick "Slapstick") member and then co-created [Tuesday](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Tuesday "Tuesday"), a three former Slapstick member [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") band, and later on one of the Alkaline Trio drummers, Mike Felumlee, was a second degree [Slapstick](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick "Slapstick") family member having played for Slapstick family member bands [Duvall](https://punkdatabase.com/w/index.php?title=Duvall&action=edit&redlink=1 "Duvall (page does not exist)") and [Smoking Popes](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Smoking_Popes "Smoking Popes") and for Alkaline Trio). They have undergone multiple lineup changes (mostly drummers) since their conception as they escalated from developing a huge local following to acquiring fame on a national level. They came together by a mutual companionship for heartbreak, angst, disdain for cops, and passion for drinking.
