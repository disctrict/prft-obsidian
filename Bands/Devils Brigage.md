## Biography
Devils Brigade is an American rock band formed as a side project by Rancid bassist [[Matt Freeman]] in 2000. In Devils Brigade Freeman performs a mix of punk rock and psychobilly styles, singing lead vocals and playing a double bass in contrast to the backing vocals and bass guitar he performs in Rancid. He was backed by his Rancid bandmates for singles released in 2003 and 2005, and recruited X drummer [[D.J. Bonebrake]] to play on Devils Brigade's eponymous debut album in 2010.
