## Biography
Josh Caterer formed Duvall in August 2001 with Tom McDougal and the drummer for the [[Smoking Popes]], Mike Felumlee (which makes Duvall a 3rd degree [Slapstick Family Tree](https://punkdatabase.com/wiki/Slapstick_Family_Tree "Slapstick Family Tree") band). In August of 2002, Mike and John left the band. Eli took over on bass and the band continued as a trio. They released one full LP, Volume Density in 2003. Rob Kellenberger, formerly of [[Slapstick]] and [[Colossal]], became their drummer temporarily. In April 2008 Neil Hennessy of [[The Lawrence Arms]] became the new drummer.
