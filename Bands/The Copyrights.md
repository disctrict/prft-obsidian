## Biography
The Copyrights were formed in 2002 in Carbondale, Illinois. Before long, the punk scene was "buzzing about these kids from southern Illinois redefining pop punk with fun lyrics, big hooks, and infectious choruses that you couldn't help but sing along with."

The band is made up of [[Adam Fletcher]] (lead vocals, bass), [[Brett Hunter]] (vocals, guitar), [[Kevin Rotter]] (vocals, guitar), and [[Luke McNeill]] (drums). They are currently signed to Fat Wreck Chords. They are known for what Alternative Press calls "both the sloppy, slacker pop-punk of, say, early Green Day with the slightly more polished sheen of Teenage Bottlerocket or recent Bouncing Souls".
