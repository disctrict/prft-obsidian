## Biography
Descendents are an American punk rock band formed in Manhattan Beach, California, in 1977, by guitarist [[Frank Navetta]], bassist [[Tony Lombardo]] and drummer [[Bill Stevenson]] as a power pop/surf punk band. In 1979, they enlisted Stevenson's school friend [[Milo Aukerman]] as a singer, and reappeared as a melodic hardcore punk band, becoming a major player in the hardcore scene developing in Los Angeles at the time. They have released eight studio albums, three live albums, three compilation albums, and four EPs. Since 1986, the band's lineup has consisted of vocalist Milo Aukerman, drummer Bill Stevenson, guitarist [[Stephen Egerton]], and bassist [[Karl Alvarez]].
