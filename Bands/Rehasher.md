## Biography
Rehasher is a punk rock band based in Gainesville, Florida, signed to No Idea Records. Although started as a band, Rehasher became the solo side project of ska punk band Less Than Jake bass player/back up and lead singer [[Roger Manganelli]] as their lead singer and rhythm guitarist. The band's first line-up included Manganelli and Ryan Geis sharing lead vocals and guitars, bass guitarist [[Gui Amador]] (formerly of [[As Friends Rust]]) and drummer [[Jake Crown]].

Rehasher's third album Make the Noise was released on July 10, 2015. Following the release of Make the Noise, the band added bassist [[Tony Farah]], and drummer [[Alex Klausner]] to resume touring. Work on new recordings began in late 2017. In 2021 the band released a covers album.
