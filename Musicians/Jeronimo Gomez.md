## Biography
Jeronimo Enrique Gomez (born August 19, 1976) is an American guitarist, bass guitarist, musician, songwriter and designer. Originally from McAllen, Texas, Gomez grew up in Davie, Florida, where he notably played in the melodic hardcore band [[As Friends Rust]], the metalcore band Poison the Well, and the indie rock band The Rocking Horse Winner.
