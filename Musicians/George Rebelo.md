## Biography
George Rebelo is one of the founding members of the seminal punk band [[Hot Water Music]]. George’s drumming has set the foundation for the band's melodic yet aggressive sound with elements derived from progressive jazz and metal. He has also gone on to be a member of one of punk’s most revered bands, [[The Bouncing Souls]], and for a year (2009) became [[Against Me!]]'s drummer during Hot Water Music's hiatus.
