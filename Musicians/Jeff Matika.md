## Biography
Jeff Matika is a friend of [[Jason White]]'s from Little Rock, who has played in several other bands - most notably Ashtray Babyhead, Magic Cropdusters, and [[Banner Pilot]]. He plays guitar and bass and sings.

He auditioned in Oakland and was selected to support [[Green Day]] on their 21st Century Breakdown tour. Jeff toured with the band and hosted Idiot Nation's The Jeff Matika Show from 2009 until 2019.
