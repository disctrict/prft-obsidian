## Biography
Neil Kevin Hennessy (born December 12, 1978) is a musician and music producer/engineer from Chicago, Illinois.

Hennessy currently plays in [[The Lawrence Arms]], [[Colossal]], [[The Falcon]], [[Smoking Popes]] and Demon Beach. He has also been a member of Baxter, The Killing Tree, and Quattro.

Hennessy works at Atlas Studios in Chicago with Matt Allison. He co-wrote the Rise Against song "Swing Life Away".
