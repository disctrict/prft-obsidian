## Biography
Derron "The Nuhf" Nuhfer was Less Than Jake's saxophonist from 1995 to 2000. Initially he was going to replace Buddy Schaub while he was touring, but he got to be a permanent member of the band until he left on 2000 after recording Borders & Boundaries. He was featured in the live shows of Losing Streak, Hello Rockview and Borders & Boundaries in 2007.
