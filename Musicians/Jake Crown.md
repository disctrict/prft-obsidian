## Biography
Jake Crown handled drums and backing vocals as an original member of [[Rehasher]] starting in 2002 until after 2008, contributing to the early recordings including full drum duties on High Speed Access to My Brain, which was tracked in just a couple of days; he departed following the band's post-2008 hiatus, after which Rehasher evolved more into a solo project for Lima.
