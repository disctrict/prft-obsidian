## Biography
Gui Amador, who had previously played bass in the band [[As Friends Rust]], joined as [[Rehasher]]'s bassist in 2002 and performed on both Off Key Melodies and High Speed Access to My Brain, providing the rhythmic foundation for the group's fast-paced punk recordings during this era. His tenure ended around 2009 amid the band's shift toward sporadic activity.
