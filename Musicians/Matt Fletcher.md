## Biography
Through his work as a member of such bands as [[Shai Hulud]], Life Force, and ONE, Matt Fletcher has been around the hardcore scene for decades playing bass around the globe. At ONE Matt does vocals.

Matt also does work at Thunderbird Clubhouse, a mental health resource center that intersects with his passion for community and touring life.
