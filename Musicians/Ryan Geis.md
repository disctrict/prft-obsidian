## Biography
Ryan Geis served as lead guitarist and backing vocalist from the [[Rehasher]]'s formation in 2002 until around 2009, contributing vocals on the debut album Off Key Melodies (2003) and select tracks on the follow-up High Speed Access to My Brain (2008), including lead vocals on "Saving Face"; he left around 2009 to pursue other musical projects.
