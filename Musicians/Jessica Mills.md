## Biography
Jessica Mills was Less Than Jake's first saxophonist. She was one of the original members from 1992 to 1997, when she left to pursue teaching and become a mother. In her abscence, trombonist Lars Nylander from Skankin' Pickle served as fill-in until 1998, when Pete Anna joined in.
