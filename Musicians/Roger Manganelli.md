## Biography
Roger Manganelli, also known as Roger Lima (born October 31st, 1973) is the bassist and one of the two singers for ska-punk band [[Less Than Jake]]. He is also lead singer and guitarist for his other band, the faster punk rock band [[Rehasher]], and more recently formed the trio Greenhorn (for whom he plays drums). When not playing with a band, Lima is either working with toys and collectibles (Pez and Garbage Pail Kids are among his favorites), flying model airplanes or drones, or developing his home studio Moathouse Productions. He also streams his playing of Fortnite on Twitch, sometimes with his son, who uses the in-game name BaconCat. He also streams on Whatnot where he mostly sells his comic books and pez.

Roger's employment history includes working at Knuckleheads, a smoke shop in Gainesville, Florida which was later transformed into a similar establishment named Smoke before going out of business.
