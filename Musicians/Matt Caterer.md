## Biography
Matt Caterer is a bassist and original member of the Chicago punk rock band [[Smoking Popes]], formed in 1990 with his brothers Josh and Eli. He has been a consistent member of the band, playing on major releases such as _Born to Quit_ and _Into the Agony_.

**Musical career and highlights**
- **Formation:** In 1990, he formed the band Speedstick with his brothers. After a brief period, they transitioned into the Smoking Popes, named after a phrase from the film _The Pope of Greenwich Village_.
- **Role:** While originally a drummer in early iterations, Matt switched to bass to allow the band to function, becoming a core songwriter and member.
- **Smoking Popes:** He played on the band's seminal 1990s albums, including _Born to Quit_ (1995), which helped propel them to national recognition.
- **Reunions:** He continued to play with the band through various reunions and recordings, including _This Is Only a Test_ (2011) and _Into the Agony_ (2018).
